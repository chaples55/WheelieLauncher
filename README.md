# Wheelie Launcher

An Android home launcher built with a **music-first** mindset for digital audio players (DAPs) and other devices with small screens.

Instead of a dense grid of apps, Wheelie puts now-playing front and center, with a circular dock of favorites around it—easy to hit with a thumb and readable at a glance while you’re listening.

## Who it’s for

- Portable DAPs and music-oriented Android devices
- Small displays where traditional launchers feel cramped
- Anyone who wants the home screen to behave like a listening console, not a phone desktop

## Features

### Now Playing
- Large center artwork from your active music app (via notification access)
- Playback progress ring, optional battery arc, and scrolling track info
- Tap to open the player; optional on-screen skip controls
- Optional EQ shortcut to your preferred equalizer app
- Blurred album art becomes wallpaper

### Circular dock
- Favorite apps arranged on a ring around Now Playing
- Long-press and drag to rearrange with live spring motion and wobble
- Long-press for remove; customize labels and icons (icon packs or gallery)
- Adjustable icon size and ring radius for different screen sizes

### App drawer
- Pull up (or use the dock button) for a searchable app list
- Transparent sheet over the home wallpaper
- Hide apps, add to dock, uninstall, and other quick actions
- Configurable columns, icon size, labels, search, and dim strength

### Look & feel
- Uses the system wallpaper when nothing is playing
- Optional clock, status bar, and scrim controls
- Icon pack support
- Individual icon picker

## Requirements

- Android 12+ (API 31)
- Set Wheelie as the default home app
- Grant notification access for Now Playing metadata and controls
