# Privacy Policy for Wheelie Launcher

**Last updated:** August 6, 2026  
**Developer:** AcousticFish  
**Contact:** Open an issue at [github.com/chaples55/WheelieLauncher](https://github.com/chaples55/WheelieLauncher/issues)

## Overview

Wheelie Launcher (“the App”) is an Android home screen launcher designed for local use on your device. We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share your personal information. The App does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs that transmit data to us.

## Data the App uses on your device

The App processes information **only on your device** to provide launcher and music features, for example:

- Installed apps and shortcuts (to display the dock, drawer, and launch apps)
- Media session / notification information you authorize (to show Now Playing artwork, titles, and playback controls)
- Settings, dock layout, icon customizations, and similar preferences (stored locally on the device)
- Images you choose for wallpaper or custom icons (handled locally; system wallpaper is shown through the Android wallpaper APIs)

This information is **not uploaded** to us.

## Permissions

The App may request permissions required for a launcher and Now Playing experience (such as notification access). Those permissions are used only for the features described above.

## Children

The App does not target children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the [Wheelie Launcher GitHub repository](https://github.com/chaples55/WheelieLauncher/issues).
